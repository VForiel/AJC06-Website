Why AJC06 exists

Our missions

AJC06 supports, guides and promotes PhD candidates and early-career researchers in the region through events, initiatives and partnerships, for their personal, academic and professional growth.

01

Science and culture

Bringing doctoral life at Université Côte d'Azur to life

  • Social events: concerts, hikes, PhD nights out
  • Taking part in the Fête de la science
  • Supporting PhD student associations at Université Côte d'Azur
02

Communication

Sharing information between PhD candidates

  • Announcements and chat on the WhatsApp community, news on Instagram, LinkedIn and this website
  • Useful links gathered in the Resources section
03

Careers

Promoting research training and preparing life after the PhD

  • Career workshops: entrepreneurship, industry, academia…
  • Member of the BioTechno network, which promotes doctoral training and works on the employability of young life-science graduates
  • Member of the Scientific and Steering Council of OTECCA (Observatoire de la transition écologique et citoyenne Côte d'Azur)
04

Representation

Representing and defending early-career researchers in the Alpes-Maritimes

  • Dialogue with Université Côte d'Azur and student unions
  • Local and national advocacy for better working conditions for early-career researchers
  • Practical guide to PhD candidates' rights

Support us

Every donation, however small, counts: it is then multiplied by public grants.

€1 = lots of €

A €1 donation can help us obtain €5, €10 or even €50 in public grants (it depends on several factors, but you get the idea). The same goes for donations in kind: getting to one of our events by your own means, or giving us equipment you no longer need.

€1 = €0.33 for you

AJC06 is recognised as an association of general interest: if you pay income tax in France, your donations, including in kind, give you a tax reduction of 66% of their amount.

How is the money managed?

The association is funded from three sources:

  • Public grants, which fund the most expensive events under an agreement with the funding body.
  • Donations and membership fees, which give the association its own funds and a little freedom: a free drink, hosting this website…
  • Merchandise sales and event fees, which lower the cost of our events.

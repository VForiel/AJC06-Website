A few pointers to find your way as a PhD candidate at [Université Côte d'Azur](https://univ-cotedazur.fr/).

## Doctoral schools

The doctoral school (ED) organises the PhD within universities and grandes écoles, together with the laboratories that host PhD candidates. It handles enrolment and re-enrolment, follows the thesis work alongside the supervisor and organises the training courses taken during the PhD.

Université Côte d'Azur has six:

- **[ED SHAL](https://edlshs.unice.fr/fr/accueil)**: humanities, arts and literature
- **[ED STIC](https://edstic.univ-cotedazur.fr/fr/node/1)**: information and communication sciences and technologies
- **[ED SFA](https://adum.fr/as/ed/sfa/home.pl)**: fundamental and applied sciences
- **[ED DESPEG](https://ed-despeg-unice.fr/)**: law, political science, economics and management
- **[ED SVS](http://www.unice.fr/ed-svs/index.php)**: life and health sciences
- **ED SMH**: human movement sciences

Looking for a research job can be an exciting adventure, and a real challenge. These websites will help.

## Find A Post Doc — postdocs

Help with **finding a postdoc**, by country and discipline. As a bonus, a **forum full of advice**: visas, legislation, relationships with your supervisor, copyright…

[findapostdoc.com](https://www.findapostdoc.com/)

## Emploi scientifique en France — civil service

Civil-service vacancies in France (secondments, assignments, competitive exams): researchers, lecturer-researchers, research engineers. Browse the offers and upload your CV. In French.

[emploi-scientifique.info](https://www.emploi-scientifique.info/)

## ABG — job offers

**Job offers** in France and worldwide, **CV upload**. ABG also runs **training courses** and offers **personalised coaching**.

[abg.asso.fr](https://www.abg.asso.fr/fr/)

## Euraxess — research in Europe

**Research job offers** across Europe, help finding funding, practical advice for living in European countries.

[euraxess.ec.europa.eu](https://euraxess.ec.europa.eu/)

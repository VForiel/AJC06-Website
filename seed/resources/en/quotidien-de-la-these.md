A PhD starts with intensive research: exploring the literature, analysing previous work, identifying the gaps to fill. That is how the conceptual foundations of the thesis are laid.

## Writing your thesis

Writing up unfolds over weeks and months. Every day is a chance to turn abstract ideas into a structured, well-argued text. It takes patience and perseverance: rephrasing, refining concepts, looking for the best way to pass on what you have learned.

## Sharing your thesis online

The Centre pour la communication scientifique directe (CCSD) explains what is at stake in open-access publication (in French): [sharing your thesis in open access](https://www.ccsd.cnrs.fr/2017/04/diffuser-sa-these-en-libre-acces-les-enjeux/).

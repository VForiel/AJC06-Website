## Confédération des jeunes chercheurs (CJC)

- A national, multidisciplinary non-profit, the CJC brings together associations of PhD candidates and doctors working to **promote the PhD** and its recognition as professional experience.
- Nationally, it is a **key contact for public authorities** (government, Parliament, CNESER…) and for any organisation wishing to discuss these issues.
- At European level, it contributes to the debate on the PhD through the [Eurodoc](https://www.eurodoc.net/) federation, of which it is a founding member.

Some CJC resources (in French): [the PhD under the microscope](https://cjc.jeunes-chercheurs.org/doctorat-a-la-loupe/), [the doctoral contract](https://cjc.jeunes-chercheurs.org/expertise/contrat-doctoral/), [FAQ for international PhD candidates](https://cjc.jeunes-chercheurs.org/faq/rubrique.php?r=etranger).

## The BIOTechno network

- The BIOTechno network of young life-science researchers federates associations of PhD candidates and doctors in biotechnology across France.
- It aims to **promote doctoral training** and **improve the employability of young graduates**, whether they create knowledge (research), share it (teaching) or innovate (start-ups).
- It works with ministries, companies and public research bodies, and organises the **BIOTechno Days** in several major cities to showcase careers in the sector.

AJC06 is a member of the network. [BIOTechno network website](https://www.reseau-biotechno.com/)

## ADCIFRE SHS

The association of PhD candidates and doctors on CIFRE agreements in the humanities and social sciences, founded in June 2014, is:

- an **independent, collaborative space** to reflect on the practical and scientific issues of CIFRE status in the humanities and social sciences;
- a **forum for debate between researchers, laboratories and institutions** on research in these fields, including research funded by its own field;
- an **association open to all young researchers** (master's students, PhD candidates, doctors) who want to join this collective reflection.

[ADCIFRE SHS website](https://adcifreshs.wixsite.com/adcifreshs)
